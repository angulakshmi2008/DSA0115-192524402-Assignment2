2. Performance Prediction (transaction_benchmark.cpp)
- Measure: Elapsed time (milliseconds) across 100,000, 1,000,000, and 5,000,000 additions
  with identical input streams and verified identical final snapshots.
- Expected complexity:
  - Original shifting array: O(k) per insertion after buffer fills (shifts k - 1 = 9 items).
  - Circular buffer: O(1) per insertion (direct write + start/count index update).
- Expected measured behaviour:
  - At -O0 (unoptimized): Circular buffer should be noticeably faster than shifting array
    across all three counts (100k, 1M, 5M) because the inner 9-iteration loop is skipped.
  - At -O2 (optimized): For small k = 10, the compiler may unroll or vectorize the 9-element
    shift in registers/L1 cache, so the speedup ratio may narrow compared to -O0, whereas
    for larger k = 50 the O(k) vs O(1) separation should remain much clearer.
  - Both versions should scale approximately linearly with N (5M taking roughly 5x 1M and
    50x 100k).
